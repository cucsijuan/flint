# Outline and bookmarks

## Outline

The outline tab in the right sidebar lists this note's headings and updates while you type. Click one to jump to it.

## Bookmarks

The bookmarks tab in the left sidebar already has a note, a heading and a search. Drag them to reorder.

Bookmark more from a note's header, a heading in the outline, or the search panel.

### A heading to bookmark

Hover it in the outline and click the bookmark icon.
